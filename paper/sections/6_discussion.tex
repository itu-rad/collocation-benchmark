\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{When does decomposition pay?} The topology study is designed to
give a systems answer rather than an ML one: if decomposition helps, the
controls locate the help --- graph structure (Shared retains it) or
physical residency (only Decomposed has it) --- and the cost side is
resident memory in proportion to instance count. The shared-instance
control doubles as a diagnostic practitioners can run on their own
workloads, with the batch-1 scoping of \S\ref{sec:setup-topology}
attached.

\paragraph{What collocation-aware measurement reveals.} The staged study's
descent --- from ``the indexer slows my service'' toward a dose--response
priced in bytes per second --- is only expressible because collocation,
per-process attribution, and reconfiguration are first-class. Whether the
descent confirms H1 on these testbeds is a pending measurement
(\S\ref{sec:results-staged}); the method is indifferent to the verdict.
If H1 survives, the deliverable is a platform-scoped bandwidth pricing of
interference; if it falls to engine-dependent slopes, the same
instrumentation localizes contention in engine-specific paths instead. On
platforms with different contention surfaces (discrete-GPU time slicing,
storage) the staged method applies with different co-runners. We offer the
method as the contribution and the unified-memory study as its first
instantiation.

\paragraph{Instrument honesty.} Several of our findings began as
instrument errors --- among them a pooled bootstrap that hid a contaminated
run and an append-mode log capable of flipping an overhead's sign
(\S\ref{sec:results-fidelity} references the same incidents). Each survives
as a guardrail in the released methodology (hierarchical statistics with
printed run values; session-aware trace parsing; environment hard-gates): a
measurement paper should show its failure modes, not only its results.

\paragraph{Limitations.} \sysname{} is single-node by design --- the
distributed machinery of production engines is precisely the confound a
measurement instrument must not carry (\S\ref{sec:framework}); distributed
pipelines are future work. Stages within a pipeline share a Python
interpreter, so pure-Python stages serialize on the interpreter lock ---
acceptable for kernel-bound ML stages, and bounded by CAL-1, but a real
constraint for CPU-heavy custom logic, which should drop into native code or
a separate pipeline. Per-device occupancy is residency, not utilization;
causal attribution depends on platform counters, which on GB10's current
driver stack lack a calibrated DRAM channel (our law is proxy-backed there).
Quality evaluation inherits the limits of EM/F1 on generative answers even
with the short-answer protocol; and our two testbeds, chosen to make
contention vivid, mean the bandwidth findings should be generalized to
discrete-memory servers only with new measurements, not by extrapolation.
