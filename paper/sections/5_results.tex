\paragraph{Step B --- intensity, isolated by one loadgen edit.}
Figure~\ref{fig:staged-diffs} shows the entire transition.
\pending{Foreground degradation vs.\ offered background rate at
$\{25,50,75,100\}\%$ of the indexer's maximum; the counters convert the
axis to bytes/s.} The hypothesis is monotone growth; a flat response would
bound the platform's tolerance for this co-runner and would be reported as
exactly that.

\paragraph{Step C --- is degradation priced in traffic or in identity?}
One stage-list edit replaces the indexer with single-resource co-runners
(CPU stream, GPU encode, ANE encode) at matched traffic ladders.
\pending{Per-engine dose--response slopes below the saturation knee, with
CIs.} H1 predicts statistically compatible slopes at matched bytes/s;
engine-dependent slopes would instead localize contention in
engine-specific paths and would redirect the analysis --- both outcomes
are wired into this subsection.

\paragraph{Step D --- the counter-free control.} One foreground edit
reduces serving to bare decode, split by phase. H2 predicts per-token
decode (bandwidth-heavy) degrades with co-runner traffic while
time-to-first-token (compute-heavy prefill) moves substantially less; the
asymmetry is directional, not absolute (\S\ref{sec:setup-staged}).
\pending{Phase-split slopes; the closing overlay of Step-A system points
on the Step-C/D dose--response.} On the M2~Pro the analysis is
counter-backed with per-engine attribution; on GB10 it is proxy-backed,
anchored by the analytic CPU-stream co-runner, and worded with that scope.

\subsection{Instrument Fidelity}
\label{sec:results-fidelity}

\paragraph{CAL-1: harness overhead (M2~Pro).} Table~\ref{tab:noop}
summarizes. Fitting per-query latency against depth separates a fixed
per-query cost of ${\approx}\SI{25}{\micro\second}$ (intercept) from a
\emph{marginal} per-stage cost of \SI{60.3}{\micro\second} (slope), flat
across two orders of magnitude of depth: dispatch does not accumulate.
The table reports two distinct quantities and labels them accordingly:
\emph{per-stage cost} ($L_q/d$, which at small depth includes the
amortizing fixed cost --- hence 84.6 at depth 1 falling to 60.4 by depth
100) and \emph{stage self-time} (the stage's own run interval,
${\approx}\SI{31}{\micro\second}$, the remainder being queue hand-off and
scheduling). Reference passing is payload-independent
(\SI{30.8}{\micro\second} at zero vs.\ \SI{31.6}{\micro\second} at
\SI{10}{\mebi\byte}) while the deep-copy counterfactual grows linearly to
\SI{1059}{\micro\second} --- a $33\times$ gap at \SI{10}{\mebi\byte} that
is the measured value of the zero-copy data path. Results are robust to
the warm-up policy (under $1.5\%$ shift between 1- and 22-query drops).
Scope: against the multi-second LLM stages of
\S\ref{sec:results-topology}, dispatch is four orders of magnitude below
the measured work; against millisecond-scale vision inference it is
percent-level, and \S\ref{sec:results-reduction} accounts for it there
explicitly.

\paragraph{CAL-2: modularity overhead.} On the M2~Pro, wrapping the
EfficientNetV2-S fine-tune in the full graph-queue-thread structure
changes per-step latency by $-43\,\si{\micro\second}$ [$-262, +122$],
i.e.\ $-0.05\%$ [$-0.29, +0.14$] of a \SI{89.2}{\milli\second} step
(paired, 10 interleaved runs; per-run medians
\SI{89.1}{}--\SI{91.0}{\milli\second}) --- indistinguishable from zero at
$\pm 0.3\%$ resolution. The tracing layer adds $+2.1\%$ on this workload ---
${\approx}\SI{1.9}{\milli\second}$ over three spans per stage per step,
i.e.\ a per-\emph{span} cost, so arms with more stages pay
proportionally more; against the multi-second queries of the case studies
(which run with tracing enabled) even the many-stage arms' span cost is
${<}0.1\%$ and cannot reorder comparisons, and the millisecond-scale
reduction cells run with tracing disabled so the span cost never touches
them. We report the negative point estimate
deliberately: a wrapper can only add work, so the honest reading is
``below the noise floor,'' and the artifact documents the two instrument
confounds this stance caught. \pending{GB10 counterpart.}
